## Common questions

**Online play says it can't reach the server.**
The game server sleeps when nobody is playing. The first connection can take up to a minute while it wakes up. Leave the screen open and it connects on its own.

**My opponent left.**
If a player disconnects, their seat is held for 30 seconds. The game pauses and every cooldown freezes while they are away. If they don't come back, the game ends and you win by resignation.

**How do I play with a friend?**
Choose Play Online, create a private room and send your friend the six-character room code. They choose Join room and enter it.

**What are the rules?**
There are no turns: move any piece that is ready. A piece that just moved cools down before it can move again, and you win by capturing the king. Tap "How to play" on the home screen for the full rules.
